Your words are chosen for your major. You can change this at any time; your progress for each major is kept.

Print vocabulary cards

Ten cards on one sheet, with the meanings on the back. In your printer settings choose double-sided, then cut along the dashed lines.

Most printers flip on the long edge. If the meanings come out behind the wrong words, print again with the other setting.
