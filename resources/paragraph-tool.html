Sentence Type Reference ▾
Topic Sentence

States the main idea of the paragraph. Usually the first sentence. Tells the reader what the whole paragraph is about.

Supporting Idea

Introduces a reason, point, or aspect that develops the topic sentence. Explains the "how" or "why" without giving specific evidence yet.

Detail / Evidence

Provides specific facts, examples, statistics, or quotations that back up a supporting idea.

Conclusion

Closes the paragraph by restating the main idea in different words or drawing a final inference from the evidence.
